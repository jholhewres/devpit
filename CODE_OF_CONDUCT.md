# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not
people.

## Not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, unwelcome attention, publishing
private information, and deliberately derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

Report privately to the maintainer ([@jholhewres](https://github.com/jholhewres)).
Reports stay private. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that
break this, and may ban repeat offenders.

Enforcement is proportionate: for most issues a word is enough, and it usually
is.
